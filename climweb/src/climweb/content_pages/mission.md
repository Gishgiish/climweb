# Our Mission

To foster participatory and inclusive community development by building capacities, mobilizing resources, and providing education and information that support sustainable, climate-resilient and equitable social, cultural, economic, and government outcomes.

## What We Do

- Building community capacities for climate adaptation
- Mobilizing resources for sustainable development
- Providing education and information on climate resilience
- Supporting equitable social and economic outcomes
- Advancing cultural and governance well-being
